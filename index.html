👤 About me

  • I am a Senior Research Fellow in mathematics at the Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore, working towards my PhD under the supervision of Dr. Soumyashant Nayak. My research focuses on operator algebras, quantum information theory and matrix analysis.

  • Before joining ISI Bangalore, I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (2021–2023), where I carried out my master's project under the supervision of Prof. Parasar Mohanty. I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Fakir Chand College, University of Calcutta (2017–2020).

⚛️ Research

My research interests are:

  • Operator algebras

  • Quantum information theory

  • Matrix analysis

At present, I am working on problems arising from the work of Prof. K. R. Parthasarathy.
