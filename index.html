Hello, I’m Somnath.

I am a Senior Research Fellow in mathematics at the Stat-Math Unit, Indian Statistical Institute, Bangalore, working towards my PhD under the supervision of Dr. Soumyashant Nayak.

My research interests lie in operator algebras, quantum information theory and matrix analysis. At present, I am working on problems arising from the work of Prof. K. R. Parthasarathy.

Before joining ISI Bangalore, I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur (2021–2023), where I carried out my master’s project under the supervision of Prof. Parasar Mohanty. I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics from Fakir Chand College, University of Calcutta (2017–2020).
